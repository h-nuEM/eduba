## Traduction

Column 1

He who built the brewery of Ningirsuk, whose god is Surmus, Entemena, his beloved king, for Ningirsu

Column 2

Entamena, for Ningirsu, his beloved Master, build the brewery.

Column 2

He built the temple of the father of Im-sag.

He built the temple of Gatumdug.

For Nanše he built 'Chosen by the heart'.

He built his great sacred terrace.

Column 4

For Ninhursaĝ he built the terrace of the sacred forest.
